About Photo Booth

Photo Booth brings the classic arcade photo strip experience straight to your browser. No downloads or bulky machines needed—just strike a pose, customize your strip, and keep your memories forever.

  1. 01

    Strike 3 Poses

    Use your webcam with a 3-second countdown per shot so you and your friends are always ready.

  2. 02

    Customize Strip

    Pick your favorite frame color, add a fun caption, and stamp the date on your 3-photo strip.

  3. 03

    Cloud Gallery

    Save your finished strips to your personal account or download them anytime to share.
